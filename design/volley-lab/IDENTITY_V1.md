# Volley Lab — Identity V1 (design review)

Status: **working design exploration, not approved production branding**.

## Source of truth
- Hero photography: original user-supplied **IMG_1332.jpeg**, beach volleyball photograph taken in Marblehead, Massachusetts. Preserve source pixels and full-resolution original; apply wordmarks as separate editable overlays.
- Three candidate compositions: Heritage Oval; Open Monogram; Wordmark Only.
- Keep **VOLLEY LAB** as the brand name; no location, affiliation, institution or fund claims in the permanent identity.
- On photographic teaser use **Materials / Women's Health / Venture Research** once. “Field Notes” may name an editorial research series, not serve as the permanent tagline.
- Typographic direction: traditional sporting-club serif, precise optical kerning, carefully drawn V/L without obvious university mimicry.
- Palette concept: maroon/oxblood, ivory, restrained mauve; purple belongs to the photograph, not necessarily the master palette.
- Avoid stars, invented establishment dates, winged volleyball clip art, untested collegiate marks, decorative graphs, broad “sport” and physiology-coded “performance” language.
- Scientific credibility comes from documented protocols, real figures, versioned evidence and source citations—not ornamental scientific graphics.

## Production acceptance checks
1. Distinctiveness from LOVB/Gretel, athletic-club marks and known league identities.
2. Wordmark longevity independent of trendy effects or photographic assets.
3. Research-document usability: legible data labels, citations, figures and accessibility.
4. Authentic photography; no scene regeneration or synthetic content substituted as original.
5. Maintainability: SVG artwork, licensing record, clear file naming, reusable tokens.
